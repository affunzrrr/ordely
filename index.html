How it works.

  1. Bring us a find

    Choose an approved brand and tell us what you want. We estimate the full cost in LKR, including customs and delivery.

  2. Place a pre-order

    Confirm your details, then pay a 50% deposit to secure your order before the batch closes.

  3. Settle after customs

    We buy on your behalf and consolidate shipments. Once customs clears your order, pay the final balance for local delivery.
